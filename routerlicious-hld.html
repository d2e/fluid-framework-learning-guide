ROUTERLICIOUS COMPLETE HLD FLOW — 4 SECTIONS
DELI
Monotonic Sequence Authority
  • Consumes unsequenced operations from the Kafka raw-deltas topic.
  • Assigns a per-document monotonic sequence number and minimum sequence number (MSN).
  • Publishes sequenced operations to Kafka output topic and triggers Redis broadcast.
SCRIBE
Snapshot Materializer & Checkpointer
  • Listens for inbound summary operations produced by the elected Client Summarizer.
  • Validates summary trees and commits snapshots to Historian / Git REST storage.
  • Creates durable checkpoints enabling new joining clients to bootstrap rapidly.
SCRIPTORIUM
Ordered Operation Ledger
  • Consumes sequenced operations asynchronously from Kafka.
  • Bulk-inserts ordered operations into the MongoDB deltas collection.
  • Handles historical op queries for clients reconnecting or catching up.
TOTAL ORDER BROADCAST
Decoupled Scaling Pattern
  • Decouples state sequencing (Deli) from durable op persistence (Scriptorium).
  • Clients optimistically apply local ops, then deterministically reconcile upon broadcast ack.
  • Ensures strong eventual consistency across all distributed participants.
